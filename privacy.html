Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how PersonalInjuryLawyerHub.com ("we," "us," or the "Site") collects, uses, and shares information when you visit the Site.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

If you contact us through the Site, we receive the information you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, and the contents of your message. We use this information only to respond to your inquiry.

Information Collected Automatically

Like most websites, the Site and its service providers may automatically collect standard technical information, including your IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited, and referring URLs. This information is used for site operation, security, and aggregate analytics.

Location Information

With your browser's permission, the homepage may request your device's approximate location to show you nearby personal injury lawyers and sort listings by distance. This happens entirely in your browser: your coordinates are compared against our listing data on your device and are not transmitted to or stored on our servers. You can decline this permission in your browser and still use the full directory, including the zip code search.

Lead Inquiry Forms

If you submit an "Inquire" form to request a lawyer match, we collect the information you provide (such as your name, contact details, zip code, and case details) and share it with our lead-management system so that a matching attorney can follow up with you. Submitting this form does not create an attorney-client relationship.

Cookies and Advertising

This Site displays advertisements served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this Site and other sites on the Internet.

  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can learn more about how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • You may also opt out of some third-party cookies at www.aboutads.info.

Third-Party Services

The Site uses third-party services to function, including map tile providers (such as OpenStreetMap and Esri) and a zip code lookup service. When the Site loads content from these providers, your browser sends standard technical information (such as your IP address) to them. Their handling of that information is governed by their own privacy policies.

How We Use Information

  • To operate, maintain, and improve the Site
  • To respond to inquiries and requests
  • To monitor for abuse and protect the security of the Site
  • To comply with legal obligations

Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers who help us operate the Site, when required by law, or in connection with a business transfer.

Data About Listed Businesses

Directory listings contain business information compiled from publicly available sources. If you represent a listed business and wish to correct or remove your listing, please use our Contact page.

Children's Privacy

The Site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your Choices

You may disable cookies in your browser settings, though some features of the Site may not function properly. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights regarding your personal information, such as the right to access or delete it. To exercise any such right, contact us using the details below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the Site after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy may be sent through our Contact page or emailed to contact@personalinjurylawyerhub.com.
