What a motorcycle accident attorney handles

Motorcycle accident claims involve riders who, without the protection of a vehicle frame, tend to sustain far more serious injuries than occupants of cars in comparable collisions. These cases also carry a distinct challenge: rider bias, the tendency of adjusters and jurors to assume the motorcyclist was speeding or riding recklessly.

Common motorcycle accident attorney cases in Cherry Hill Township, New Jersey

  • Left-turn collisions where a driver turns across a rider's path
  • Lane-change crashes caused by drivers failing to check blind spots
  • Collisions caused by road hazards, debris, or poorly maintained pavement
  • Rear-end impacts at stops and in slow traffic
  • Crashes involving vehicles opening doors into a rider's lane

How these claims are proven

Countering assumptions about the rider is a real part of these cases. Helmet use, protective gear, licensing and training records, and speed evidence from scene analysis all matter. Physical evidence tends to carry disproportionate weight: gouge marks, debris fields, and vehicle damage patterns often establish the actual sequence more reliably than either driver's recollection.

Compensation in motorcycle accident attorney claims

Motorcycle injuries commonly include road rash requiring skin grafts, fractures, spinal injuries, and traumatic brain injuries. Claims often involve extended rehabilitation, permanent scarring, and long-term impairment, which pushes future care and lost earning capacity to the center of the valuation.

Deadlines that apply

Every state sets a statute of limitations for injury lawsuits, and claims involving a government entity often carry far shorter notice deadlines measured in months. Because those periods differ by state and by claim type, confirm the deadline that applies to your situation with a lawyer licensed in New Jersey rather than relying on a general figure. What to expect when you contact a firm

Most motorcycle accident attorneys offer a free initial consultation, and the great majority work on a contingency fee, meaning you owe no attorney fee unless they recover money for you. That first conversation is usually a screening call: they will ask what happened, when it happened, whether you have received medical treatment, and whether you have spoken with any insurer.

If the firm takes the case, the early work typically involves sending a letter of representation so adjusters contact them instead of you, gathering the police or incident report, collecting your medical records and bills, and identifying every insurance policy that might apply. They will generally wait to value the claim until your treatment stabilizes, because settling before then means settling without knowing what your future care will cost.

Before signing anything, ask who will handle your file day to day, what the fee percentage is and when it increases, whether case costs are deducted before or after the fee is calculated, and what happens to those costs if the case is unsuccessful. Get the fee agreement in writing and read it away from the office.

How much does it cost to hire a motorcycle accident attorney in Cherry Hill Township, New Jersey?

Most personal injury firms work on contingency, meaning you pay attorney fees only if they recover money for you, and initial consultations are typically free. Percentages and the treatment of case costs vary between firms, so ask for the fee agreement in writing before signing.

Do I need a lawyer for a motorcycle accident claim?

Not every claim requires representation. Minor incidents with no lasting injury and undisputed fault can often be handled directly with the insurer. Representation tends to add the most value when injuries are significant, fault is disputed, multiple parties are involved, or an insurer has denied the claim or made a low offer.
